# PetCare food visual assets

The bundled food-focused illustrations are cropped from a generated photorealistic PetCare visual mockup. They are **concept images**, not verified photographs of the exact branded products. Replace with licensed and product-matched supplier photography before a real store launch.

These local files are bundled to prevent product images falling back to animal portraits or depending on external image URLs. Food and treat SKU photos map to food types in `src/storeCatalog.js`.
